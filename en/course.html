Overview

The ATX Motorsport GT3 Championship is a French-speaking ACC competition built on consistency, pure driving skill and reliability over a long season. Each driver freely chooses their car among the homologated GT3 models — no one-make format here. The goal: a clean, readable, regular competition, with a fixed weekly meet-up and the same format every round.

  • Open GT3 category — each driver picks their car among the homologated models
  • One event every week
  • Long season of 12 rounds + 1 final
  • Consistency-based standings: the 8 best results are kept

Overview

ATX Motorsport Endurance is a series of 5 GT3 rounds, raced by crews of 2 to 3 drivers who take turns at the wheel according to the driver-change rules in force. Races last between 3 and 9 hours depending on the circuit.

  • GT3 category
  • Crews of 2 to 3 drivers with mandatory driver changes
  • 5 rounds of variable length, from 3 to 9 hours
  • Overall standings across the full season

Season format

ElementDetail
Duration5 rounds, from 3 to 9 hours depending on the circuit
CategoryGT3
Crews2 to 3 drivers per car, with driver changes during the race
StandingsOverall standings across the season

Technical regulations

  • Refuelling: mandatory pit stop(s) for fuel; the number of stops depends on consumption and race duration, at each crew's discretion
  • Tyres: free tyre changes, not mandatory, at each crew's discretion
  • Driver changes: at least one mandatory driver change during the race, only during a pit stop with the engine off; maximum stint length follows the current official FIA rules

Points system

The scale follows the F1 base (25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1), multiplied by the number of hours in the round.

Overview

The ATX Motorsport Porsche Cup is a one-make cup raced exclusively on the Porsche 992 GT3 R: every driver runs the same car with the same homologated setup, putting pure driving skill front and center. Each weekend follows a Formula 1-inspired format: qualifying, a reverse-grid Sprint race, then a main race on the qualifying grid.

  • One-make — Porsche 992 GT3 R for everyone
  • Same homologated setup for every driver
  • Official liveries
  • Double-race format: Sprint (reverse grid) + Main race (qualifying grid)
  • Short, intense season: 5 rounds + 1 final
