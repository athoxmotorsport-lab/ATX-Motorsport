ABOUT ATX MOTORSPORT

More than a community,
a team.

ATX Motorsport is first and foremost a French-speaking team dedicated to Assetto Corsa Competizione, backed by a community of committed drivers. Our goal is to bring together drivers of all levels around a shared passion: motorsport, progression, and the pleasure of racing together in a friendly, competitive atmosphere.

Our Mission

Building a lasting team where every driver finds their place.

ATX Motorsport aims to provide a structured environment where both beginners and experienced drivers can improve. We organize events, develop custom liveries, share our experience and do everything we can to build a genuine team.

Our Values

Passion

Every race is driven by our passion for motorsport and sim racing.

Competition

Events organized seriously to deliver quality racing.

Respect

Fair play and good spirits are essential to who we are.

Progression

We move forward together to get better, race after race.

Our Philosophy

"Performance isn't just about lap times."

It's built through hard work, shared experience, respect for fellow drivers, and the pleasure of racing every event with seriousness and good spirit.

Join the adventure.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced driver, you'll find your place within ATX Motorsport.
